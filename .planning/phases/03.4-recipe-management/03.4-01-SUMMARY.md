## Decisions Made
- save-as-new appends " (edited)" suffix to forked recipe name for clear differentiation
- update-all cascades only to entries where no ingredients have user_modified=1 (respects user edits)
- UxMode default is 'confirm-only' -- safest for food tracking accuracy (not 'zero-effort')
- recipe_name is optional on VlmDish -- only Gemini Nano populates it; SmolVLM path unchanged
