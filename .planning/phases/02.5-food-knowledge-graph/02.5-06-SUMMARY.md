## Decisions Made
- MIN_RECIPE_COUNT=10 threshold: >= 3 produced 78K dishes / 295MB (too large); >= 10 produces 15.9K recipe dishes / 58MB, meeting both 15K+ dish count and 70MB size constraints
- Top 30 ingredients per dish: popular dishes like "chili" aggregated 2,400+ unique ingredient strings across all recipes; capping at 30 most frequent ingredients per dish reduced rows from 1.4M to 452K while preserving nutritional accuracy
- Confidence formula adjusted to 0.4-0.9 range since all dishes now have >= 10 recipes (no low-confidence 1-2 recipe dishes)
