Target KG schema (8 tables):
- cuisine (id, name, region)
- dish_category (id, cuisine_id, name, parent_id)
- dish (id, category_id, canonical_name, description, avg_calories/protein/carbs/fat_per_serving, default_serving_grams, source, confidence)
- dish_alias (id, dish_id, alias, language, alias_type)
- recipe (id, dish_id, name, source, total_weight_grams, servings, is_canonical)
- recipe_ingredient (id, recipe_id, usda_fdc_id, ingredient_name, quantity_grams, sort_order)
- usda_food (fdc_id, description, food_group, calories/protein/fat/carbs/fiber_per_100g)
- symspell_deletes (id, dish_id, delete_variant)
- dish_fts (FTS5 on canonical_name, description)
- dish_alias_fts (FTS5 on alias)
