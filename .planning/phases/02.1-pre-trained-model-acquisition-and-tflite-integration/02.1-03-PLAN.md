  <what-built>
Full detection pipeline with bundled pre-trained models (Google AIY Food V1 for binary gate + classification, YOLO26n COCO for detection). The APK contains the entire pipeline: photo capture -> preprocessing (640x640 + 224x224) -> binary gate -> YOLO detection -> classification -> bounding boxes with confidence indicators -> portion estimates -> detail sheet -> log meal.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Install the APK from `http://100.78.19.90:8888/foodtracker-release.apk` (or via `/tmp/claude-shared-files/foodtracker-release.apk`).

**Test 1: End-to-end detection**
1. Open the app
2. Tap the camera/detection button
3. Take a photo of food (a banana, apple, pizza, or sandwich work best since those are COCO food classes)
4. Verify: spinner appears during processing, then results screen shows bounding boxes with confidence-colored outlines (green/yellow/red)
5. Expected: YOLO26n COCO can detect banana, apple, sandwich, orange, broccoli, carrot, hot dog, pizza, donut, cake. Other foods will be detected by the binary gate but may not have specific bounding boxes.

**Test 2: Swipe-to-dismiss with undo**
1. On the results screen with detected items, swipe a detected item to dismiss
2. Verify: undo toast appears showing the food name
3. Tap "Undo"
4. Verify: item reappears in both the photo overlay and the detection list

**Test 3: Detail sheet and portion slider**
1. Tap a bounding box on the annotated photo
2. Verify: bottom sheet opens with item detail
3. Adjust the portion slider from 0.5x to 3.0x
4. Verify: weight display updates in real time without lag

**Test 4: Log meal persistence**
1. Tap the "Log Meal" FAB
2. Verify: navigates back to diary/home
3. Verify: the newly logged meal appears in the diary

**Known limitations for v1:**
- Only 10 COCO food classes have bounding box detection (banana, apple, sandwich, orange, broccoli, carrot, hot dog, pizza, donut, cake)
- Classification labels are from AIY Food V1 (2023 classes) but may show generic class indices for uncommon foods
- Portion estimates are approximate (Phase 2 proxy calculations)
