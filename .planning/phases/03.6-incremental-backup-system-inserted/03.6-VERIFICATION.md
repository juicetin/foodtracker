### Gaps Summary

Two gaps block full goal achievement:

**Gap 1 — Incomplete table coverage in TRACKED_TABLES (BKP-05 partial):**

The phase goal explicitly names "recipes" and "settings" as data to back up. `custom_recipes` and `user_settings` are defined in `schema.ts` and are used by `exportService.ts`, but neither table is in `TRACKED_TABLES` in `types.ts`. As a result, any INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on these tables will not generate journal entries, meaning incremental backups will miss recipe and settings changes. Full backups (VACUUM INTO) capture the entire DB file and are unaffected. The fix is one line: add `'custom_recipes'` and `'user_settings'` to the TRACKED_TABLES Set in `apps/mobile/src/services/backup/types.ts`.

Note: Since `db/migrations.ts` is not yet wired into App.tsx, these tables may not be created at runtime. Adding them to TRACKED_TABLES is safe regardless — the updateHook will simply never fire for them until the tables exist.

**Gap 2 — BKP requirements absent from REQUIREMENTS.md:**

BKP-01 through BKP-06 are referenced in plan frontmatter and ROADMAP.md but have no entries in REQUIREMENTS.md. The requirements registry is incomplete. This is a documentation gap that does not affect runtime behavior but breaks traceability.
