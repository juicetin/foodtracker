## Human Verification Required

### 1. Entry card tap cycle feel

**Test:** Open diary tab with at least one logged entry. Tap an entry card three times.
**Expected:** Cycles summary (macros visible) → ingredients (per-dish list) → collapsed (name+photo only). Three dots at bottom-right show current position. Transition is smooth (200ms).
**Why human:** Reanimated `withTiming` animation and dot visual affordance require runtime rendering to confirm.

### 2. Swipe date navigation vs. vertical scroll coexistence

**Test:** On a diary day with several entries, attempt to scroll vertically, then attempt to swipe horizontally.
**Expected:** Vertical scroll works normally; horizontal swipe advances/retreats date without triggering scroll.
**Why human:** `Gesture.Pan` `failOffsetY([-10, 10])` conflict behavior is runtime-only.

### 3. StickyMacroHeader pin behavior during scroll

**Test:** Log enough entries to require scrolling. Scroll down through the list.
**Expected:** Macro header (calorie number, P/C/F pills, toggle button) remains pinned at top throughout scroll.
**Why human:** "Sticky" placement outside ScrollView must be visually confirmed at runtime.

### 4. Week overview bar accuracy

**Test:** Navigate to days with and without logged entries.
**Expected:** Days with entries show green filled circles; empty days show hollow gray circles; today has a dot indicator.
**Why human:** Requires live SQLite data to confirm `entryPresence` Map correctly drives rendering.
