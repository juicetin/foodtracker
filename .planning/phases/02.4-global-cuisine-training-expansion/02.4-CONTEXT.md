# Phase 2.4: Global Cuisine Training Expansion - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-03-13
**Status:** Ready for planning
**Source:** HuggingFace research survey + additional datasets research

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Expand the food classifier from 335 to 700+ classes by downloading additional datasets, merging them into the training pipeline, retraining EfficientNet-Lite0, exporting to TFLite, and deploying to the app. This is primarily offline ML work with a model swap at the end.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Datasets to Add (prioritized)
From Kaggle/academic sources (already researched):
- **CNFOOD-241** — 241 Chinese food classes, 191K images (Kaggle/Mendeley)
- **Khana** — 80 Indian food classes, 131K images (Google Drive)
- **Taiwanese Food 101** — 101 classes, ~3.8 GB (Kaggle)
- **VietFood67** — 67 Vietnamese classes, 33K images (Kaggle)
- **THFOOD-50** — 50 Thai classes, 15.7K images (GitHub)
- **MAFood-121** — 121 classes from 11 global cuisines (Kaggle)

From HuggingFace:
- **bharat-raghunathan/indian-foods-dataset** — 15 Indian classes, 4,770 images, CC0
- **rajistics/indian_food_images** — 20 Indian classes, 6,269 images
- **Tinsae/Ethiopian-foods** — 11 Ethiopian classes, 1,097 images
- **lyan62/FoodieQA** — 350 regional Chinese food entries

### Training Approach
- Same architecture: EfficientNet-Lite0 (proven at 335 classes)
- Same training script: `/home/me/media/foodtracker-ml/train_food2k.py` (update num_classes and dataset_dir)
- Same merge strategy: symlink-based merge with dataset prefixes to avoid name collisions
- Same export pipeline: PyTorch → ONNX → TFLite INT8
- Target: >70% top-1 val accuracy on 700+ classes (harder problem, lower expected accuracy)

### Class Deduplication
- Many datasets overlap (ramen in Food-101 + UEC-256 + CNFOOD-241)
- Need a deduplication/mapping step: identify semantic duplicates across datasets
- Strategy: merge under canonical names, combine training images

### Claude's Discretion
- Which datasets to prioritize if download sizes are prohibitive
- How to handle class imbalance (some classes with 750+ images, others with <100)
- Whether to increase model capacity (EfficientNet-Lite1 or Lite2) for 700+ classes
- Data augmentation strategy for underrepresented classes

</decisions>
