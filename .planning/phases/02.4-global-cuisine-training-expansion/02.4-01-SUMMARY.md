## Decisions Made

- **Conservative fuzzy matching:** Only Levenshtein distance 1 for names >= 10 chars. Shorter names have too many false positives in food nomenclature (fried_fish vs dried_fish, green_salad vs greek_salad).
- **Manual dedup map:** Curated list of 30+ known cross-dataset food name variants (burger/hamburger, pakora/pakode, cholebhature/chole_bhature, etc.). Safer than automated fuzzy matching for food.
- **Symlink-based merge:** All merged images are os.symlink() to source datasets. Zero disk duplication for the 144K image collection.
- **Kaggle deferral:** Script gracefully skips Kaggle datasets when no auth. Re-running after setup picks up where it left off.
